# Portal Guns (Factorio 2.0)

A handheld portal gun. Left click shoots a blue portal, right click an orange one, Shift + click closes the
portal of that colour, Alt + P or the shortcut button closes both. Characters, cars and tanks (keeping their
speed), spidertrons and biters that step into one portal come out of the other. Every player has their own pair.

Ручная портальная пушка. Левый клик — синий портал, правый — оранжевый, Shift + клик закрывает портал этого
цвета, Alt + P или кнопка на панели закрывают оба. Персонажи, машины и танки (с сохранением скорости),
паукотроны и кусаки, вошедшие в один портал, выходят из другого. У каждого игрока своя пара.

- **Requires:** Factorio 2.0 (tested on 2.0.77). Space Age optional. Deterministic, built for multiplayer.
- **Unlock:** technology "Portal gun" (after Chemical science pack and Battery).
- **Install:** put `portal-guns_<version>.zip` (or this folder) into Factorio's `mods` folder.
- **Settings:** range, vehicles and units through portals, cross-surface links (off by default), owner labels.
- **Scripting:** remote interface `portal-guns` and the custom event `portal-guns-on-teleported`; see the
  repository README.
